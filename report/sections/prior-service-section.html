Federal service vs. first observed in CTMS

Service computation date and first appearance in a CTMS month are different measures. A first appearance identifies an observation interval, not an exact start date: absent in the prior snapshot, present in this one. CTMS draws heavily on federal employees who transferred in with years of prior creditable service, not just fresh hires.
