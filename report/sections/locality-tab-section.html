What this report does not claim

Observed salary differences are not labeled as locality supplements. The public OPM fields used here do not isolate whether, or by how much, a CTMS-specific talent-market adjustment contributed to an individual's reported annualized adjusted basic pay. A defensible estimate would require a publicly citable schedule or an employee-level field that separates the adjustment from other pay determinants.
