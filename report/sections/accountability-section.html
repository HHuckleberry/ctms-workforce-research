A related-but-separate program: CISA "did not properly design, implement, comply with, or manage" its Cybersecurity Retention Incentive (CRI) — $138M paid FY2020–24, incentives given to ineligible staff, no recipient records kept, $1.41M in improper back-payments to 348 people. CISA has said (per press reporting, not this PDF) it's ending CRI specifically because CTMS is meant to absorb that function — this report doesn't independently confirm that transition claim.

Context for 2025's decline

CISA's Sep–Oct 2025 exit wave in this report's own data is part of something much larger and independently reported: CISA's total workforce fell to ~2,400 by Dec 2025 (down ~29% since early 2025), following DOGE-driven contract cancellations (300+ election-security and red-team staff cut in Mar 2025) and mass departures — Axios reported a third of CISA's top cyber talent gone by Jun 2025.
